Laboratory Grade Process

Our 4-Step Purification Workflow

From raw water intake to sterile contactless bottling at our Dabra plant.

01

Multi-Micron Sediment Filtration

High-efficiency sediment pre-filters remove suspended solids, rust, silt, and coarse contaminants, shielding subsequent membrane stages.

02

High-Pressure RO Membrane

0.0001-micron semi-permeable reverse osmosis membranes eliminate heavy metals, dissolved toxic salts, nitrates, and microorganisms.

03

Alkaline Ionizer & ORP Boost

Water flows through active bio-ceramic Ionizer beds, replenishing natural calcium, magnesium, potassium ions, raising pH to 8.5 +.

04

UV & Sealed Contactless Filling

Final dual-band ultraviolet germicidal irradiation guarantees zero microbial presence before automatic pressurized clean-room sealing.
